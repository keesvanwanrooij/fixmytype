# Scope and delivery boundaries

The maintainer approved typing protection, three AI modes, dictation, narration and a companion on 2026-09-05. This supersedes the earlier exclusion of voice work and text history. History now has explicit retention controls; diagnostics still exclude content.

## Included in the first-user target

- You can choose sensitivity 1 through 5 and personal key calibration.
- You can choose AI Off, Suggest or Automatic, independently of protection.
- You can use application profiles for browser prompts, prose, code and spreadsheets.
- You can review older-sentence repairs while continuing to type.
- You can edit a local style card and vocabulary used by repair and dictation.
- You can dictate, review local history, insert into a supported target, and Undo.
- You can listen to text and enable companion observations for a chosen window.
- You can configure shortcuts and hide or show the companion.
- You can inspect local runtime setup, downloads and queue activity.

## Limits that remain binding

Passwords, elevated targets, secure desktop and unverified controls remain excluded from automatic editing. A target must advertise capabilities before the UI offers automatic edits. The user may still work in the internal editor and transfer text deliberately.

No cloud processing, telemetry, account, paid tier, kernel driver or autonomous message sending belongs in this delivery. A companion may prepare an answer but cannot submit prompts, execute terminal commands, post on social media, or change spreadsheet formulas by interpreting screen text.

## Completion

Phases 1 through 13 cover implementation, compatibility, packaging and first-user acceptance. A phase with unresolved physical checks stays open. A public release requires publication checks in addition to a working developer build.
