# Product vision

FixMyType helps people write when a damaged keyboard makes every sentence harder. The first user writes prompts in browsers, Claude Code and Codex, social posts in Word, and analyses in Excel. These contexts need different editing rules. A formula is not ordinary prose, and a terminal prompt is not a document.

## The approved experience

You can type with deterministic protection at sensitivity 1 through 5. AI has three explicit modes: Off, Suggest, and Automatic. The AI preserves your meaning and tone. You can continue writing while earlier sentences are evaluated. An older result may only change the exact passage it still owns.

You can dictate using a configurable toggle shortcut. The transcript appears in local history and can be inserted into the selected destination when that destination can be validated. You can listen to text, ask a companion about a chosen window, and prepare a reply by voice. A small companion control may stay visible or the app may live in the tray.

## What success looks like

A first-user test must demonstrate an entire supported path: start, select a mode, type or dictate, review or apply, Undo, pause, and quit. The test must include a delayed AI response arriving after later sentences were typed. Counting passing helper tests alone does not establish this result.

Every public capability must identify its tested scope. The editor can provide reliable revision tracking earlier than arbitrary external controls. Browser, Office and terminal support each need evidence before receiving the same claim.

## Delivery and sustainability

The local core stays free, Apache-2.0, without accounts or advertisements. Optional donations support maintenance and testing. The footer offers Support FixMyType without interrupting writing. The [approved ideas](24-product-workflows.md) map the ten accepted additions to delivery phases.
