# Troubleshooting

If protection feels wrong, pause it first. Add the affected app to exclusions and report a safe reproduction without private text. Do not weaken password or secure-desktop boundaries to work around compatibility.

See [README.md](../README.md), [docs hub](README.md), and [roadmap](../plans/README.md).

## Feature says unavailable

Check the setup screen for the missing local component. Keep the text as a draft and retry after the component is ready. Do not switch to an online service as a hidden fallback. Record an operation code without copying private text into the issue.

## Repair or Undo reports a conflict

The original passage or target changed while the operation was pending. Review the proposal and transfer it deliberately. Do not force the old operation over the new text. If unchanged passages conflict repeatedly, reproduce with synthetic text and report the sequence.

## Voice or companion remains active

Use Stop or global pause, then Quit from the tray. Record whether the microphone indicator, speech or observation indicator stayed active. This is a lifecycle defect, not a reason to increase permission scope. The maintainer must reproduce it before a release.
